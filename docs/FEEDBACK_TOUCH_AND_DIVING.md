# Touchscreen and fishing feedback — September 14 designer amendment

> Historical amendment/implementation record. The current approved design is consolidated in [bible.md](../bible.md). This record preserves provenance; it is not an independent authority or fallback specification.

This records the designer’s explicit follow-up to the definitive v2 Bible. The DFO request supersedes §16’s 100 m stand-off: a real patrol now waits clear of working divers, aligns behind starboard, comes alongside, and boards only after both divers are aboard and the skipper slows. The earlier simplified fictional dive-model amendment remains in force. These are game tuning choices, not real dive tables.

## Decisions and behavior

- Every one of the twelve career boats accepts the $20,000 bow-thruster retrofit. Factory thrusters remain usable. Twin-jet neutral pivot still works; the retrofit adds bow force.
- Sister boats remain beside their originals in two columns. Selecting either previews it without spending money; moving controller focus leaves that choice intact. **Buy displayed boat** names and purchases the selected hull. Owned boats stay available for comparison. Each sister has independently mixed deck capacity, fuel capacity, speed, contact depth, price, hidden maneuverability and hidden durability. Three decks are larger and three smaller. Handling affects actual turn response/rudder authority; durability affects collision damage and catastrophic-impact energy.
- Starter rudder art uses the exact supplied `harbour workhorse.png`, including its angled cabin wall. The designer explicitly confirmed: “Use that exact file as the starter exception.” This approved exception applies to the starter image; the strict-overhead requirement continues for other boats. `harbour workhorse2.png` and the old generated runtime fallback are removed from `public/` and retained under `archive/retired-boat-art/`; the runtime uses catalogue sprites or a geometric overhead placeholder. The live catalogue was visually reviewed on a labelled contact sheet. No original source art was rewritten or discarded.
- A vector arcade cabinet labelled Training Mode stands on the dock and opens the existing isolated Test Mode entrance. It is a native SVG prop, not a new raster dependency. Title and settings access remain available.
- DFO scheduling is seeded and persistent: once in days 1–2, once in days 3–12, once in days 13–22, and so on. Missed scheduled days carry to the next fishing day within that block; days spent ashore cannot host an at-sea boarding. Inspection completion prevents a second visit across sector changes/reloads. Test encounter rate zero suppresses automatic traffic; explicit training spawns remain possible. An amber direction/distance arrow shows the responding patrol. The inspection remains a configurable time cost without fines or catch removal.
- Fictional depth budgets rise to 1,200 / 300 / 165 / 110 / 80 / 60 game minutes at depth bands ≤6.5 / 12 / 18 / 22 / 30 / deeper metres. Short nitrogen-load half-life falls from 45 to 18 game minutes. All time above water—including deck preparation, bag work, harbour waits and travel—contributes to recovery. Slow repeated-day strain still persists through saves and fresh tanks.
- Normal deployment/redescent waits until the predicted nitrogen allowance covers a full bag at the intended depth. Surfaced divers use the last bag depth/rate, plus observed full-bag duration and a search/ascent allowance. Boarding and moving shallower uses local deployment depth. Hidden reckless divers retain their established refusal to follow tables. The diver-info segmented green→amber→red nitrogen meter displays load and full-bag/rest status with the reference depth. Portrait-only mode stays identity-only.
- Career air use is multiplied by 0.78; the 20-unit reserve still forces harmless ascent. The $85,000 rank-2 Nitrox system grants 60% more fictional nitrogen allowance and 20% longer air endurance, and adds one second to entry from deck. Already-surfaced bag exchange does not incur another deck entry.
- DCS hazard accumulation is halved and deterministic injury thresholds increased. The surfaced diver contact radius shrinks from 0.4 to 0.2 m, injury speed rises to 1.8 m/s, fatal overrun to 3.2 m/s, and powered-propeller fatal speed to 2.2 m/s. Fast direct strikes remain serious; underwater bubble markers are not collision targets. Boat/reef collision geometry is unchanged.
- Each diver has one major skill with full growth and two minor skills with 30% of that growth. A seeded permutation distributes all seven abilities evenly, including coverage of every ability among the four starter contacts; every seven roster positions cover every major once and every minor twice. Original identity, baseline strengths, experience, employer and orders persist.
- Early Start (05:00) is directly below Begin Working Day in the sail plan. Existing turnaround restrictions and early-passage consequences remain; sister boats use their actual drive family for that risk.
- Fit surfaced divers swim at 0.28 m/s toward nearby water with space for the boat’s full footprint when stranded on shallow ground. Wet routes avoid land; eligible pickups and ongoing recovery stop that movement. No teleport or underwater steering is added.
- Additive ground recipe v5 preserves previous reef/shelf IDs and stock. Each main sector now has 13 charted and 26 unmarked patches. All divers and rivals can harvest unmarked stock normally. Normal water/chart overlays and map hit-testing never expose their live boundaries, including after a diver’s report. Surface reports retain sampled observations in chart knowledge. **REVEAL EVERY URCHIN** is directly below Resume and toggles the patch overlay independently of other debug/assist settings; the temporary reveal is not saved.
- Easy’s current arrows render through the existing current-information setting, without debug reveal. Radio history contains broadcasts and patrol calls; local diver, bag and helm feedback stays out, including when displaying old stored logs.

## Touchscreen and browser access

Touchscreen mode is a browser-local preference available on the title, Pause and Settings. It survives reloads and career/Test Mode changes. Two independent Pointer Event controls operate throttle/bow thrust and rudder through the existing abstract command resolver. Large buttons cover deployment/boarding, bag work, recall, orders, selection, chart, zoom, ahead/reverse/neutral and rudder centring. Compass direction, quality, search time, apply and cancel all support touch. Losing a pointer, focus, visibility, viewport orientation or menu clears held contacts. Existing keyboard/controller mappings remain protected; K additionally centres the rudder.

A compact touch HUD keeps diver/nitrogen information visible. Controls use CSS touch-action only on control regions so menus can still scroll. No browser, Steam or desktop settings are changed.

`npm start -- --lan` serves only the built `dist/` directory on the local network and prints phone/tablet URLs. Default `npm start` stays loopback-only. Use the same Wi-Fi; each browser/origin stores its own career, so export/import saves when transferring devices. No public deployment, account or Firefox-only requirement is introduced.

Official implementation references: [MDN Pointer Events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Pointer_events/Using_Pointer_Events), [MDN multi-touch lifetimes](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Pointer_events/Multi-touch_interaction), [Phaser desktop/mobile browser rendering](https://docs.phaser.io/phaser/getting-started/what-is-phaser). Desktop Chromium/Firefox emulation can verify input/software routing; it cannot establish physical Android/iOS or Steam Deck performance.
