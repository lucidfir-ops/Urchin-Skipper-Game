## Decisions and compatibility

- The patch label’s bottom edge is eight screen pixels above the northernmost point of its actual rendered outline, horizontally centred over that outline. Its text remains readable across zoom levels. The camera and simulation/patch geometry do not change.
- Step 8 suggests **5–11 m in this cove**, reflecting its existing shallow terrain. After 60 seconds of active step-8 play, Frank adds: “The urchins are east of the patch you can see, right next to it!” Menus and focus/input pauses do not count. Progress is saved with the lesson and the hint remains available without repeat announcements.
- Step 9 includes the requested future upgrade and **5–25 m, gentle slope, shallower is easier, often near kelp** advice. The Recording chartplotter already retains observed ground between trips. Existing manual chart marks remain available. The Bible’s productive-ground depth limit (70 ft, about 21 m) was reported before implementation; the requested wider range is Frank’s search advice, not a change to stock generation or diving rules.
- First-boat selection, Boatyard and Chandlery share inspection and purchase behavior. Selecting a row only changes the displayed candidate. Buy appears visually within that row at its right edge, with additional top and permanent bottom **Buy selected boat/equipment** controls. Only these explicit controls open the named confirmation, initially on Cancel. Existing cash/rank/ownership/planning restrictions still govern the transaction. Buying already owned/fitted items is disabled. Current setup is reached through the distinct teal **Your boat** button.
- Boat sisters remain beside their originals. On narrow screens, the selected boat’s scrollable details expand below the pair; equipment details expand beneath its row. Larger screens keep the detail pane alongside the list. Selections and scroll state participate in Back/Forward history; payment confirmations are never replayed.
- Harbour artwork uses its original aspect ratio without a full-scene blue overlay. Destination buttons share the artwork’s coordinates: office by the left building, Frank beside his seat, crew by the divers, Boatyard over the boat, Chandlery by the right building, and Training Mode beside the arcade. Small screens pan the scene instead of reflowing targets into an unrelated grid. The header scrolls vertically with the page, so it cannot hide upper destinations; it remains visible across horizontal panning.
- The third ocean pause option opens existing Difficulty / assists. Merely opening it changes no preset. Prior touchscreen Hide UI, window scrolling/resizing, tutorial progress, physical recovery, menu history and saved careers remain supported.

## Implementation and reproduction

`shop-actions.js` holds separate candidate and transaction actions; `shop-view.js` renders two sibling buttons as one visual card (real nested buttons would break ownership/accessibility). `harbour-shops.css` owns the new layouts; obsolete touchscreen grid/wash overrides were removed. `career-intro.js` owns the hint timer/copy; `intro-view.js` presents the saved hint. `patch-style.js` computes the label anchor for `terrain-view.js`.

Run `npm run verify -- --unit-only`, then browser suites `harbour-shops`, `harbour-shops-firefox`, `hud-windows`, `hud-windows-firefox` and `controller`. The shop suite covers active versus paused hint time, label bounds, no-charge inspection, explicit cancel/confirm, setup navigation, card/purchase reachability, native Chromium scene panning, both phone orientations, 150% portrait text, sister alignment, and synthetic Xbox purchasing navigation. Fixture funds/rank allow purchase-path coverage; they are not player defaults.

Package with `python3 scripts/package-temp.py --output exports/2026-09-16-harbour-shops`. Title identity: **TEMP · SEP 16 · HARBOUR & SHOPS UPDATE**. Both ZIPs use identical game bytes. Earlier exports, checkpoints, source art and player profiles are retained. [Acceptance evidence](history/2026-09-16-harbour-shops/acceptance/README.md).
