## Decisions

- Touch menus scroll as a continuous page, including their headings, charts, detail, choices and footer. Narrow portrait views stack the columns; landscape retains useful columns. The old render loop forcibly reset outer scroll every frame: this is removed in touch mode. Browser pointer gestures remain available on menus, while helm sticks retain their own gestures. Harbour actions become a scrollable grid over the supplied scene in touch mode.
- Title offers UI scale at 80 / 90 / 100 / 110 / 125 / 150%; Settings also offers smaller, larger and reset. Browser preference survives reload independently of career. Menu and title content scale together; at-sea helm/diver text also follows the scale, with at least 44 physical pixels for touch menu buttons. Menus and sea controls finish 52 px plus the browser safe-area inset above the bottom edge. Fullscreen remains separately available. Camera projection and world geometry are unchanged.
- DFO: first scheduled visit on career day 3. Days 4–8 are clear. One seeded random day in each eight-day block beginning day 9 (9–16, 17–24, etc.). This is a career schedule, with no opening-season reset. Arrival is seeded between 11:00 and 14:00 inclusive. If the player does not fish during that window, there is no delayed backlog. Existing visits already underway finish normally. Default aboard inspection drops from 18 to 6 game minutes; explicit saved Test Mode timing remains respected. Invitation, docking and departure still work as before.
- General surface collision injury threshold rises from 1.8 to 2.8 m/s; fatal threshold from 3.2 to 4.8 m/s. Powered, exposed stern contact retains 1.8 m/s injury and 2.2 m/s fatal thresholds. Swept hull contact, relative current, angular motion, tangential brushes and underwater exclusion remain intact. These are fictional game values.
- Insurance starts at $65/trip, plus $25 per recorded diver injury and $75 per fatality. Medical/DCS injuries count. Claims are recorded in the existing once-per-trip settlement; a diver still recovering does not create another claim. Existing receipts supply migration history. Paid cover is fixed for the current trip; subsequent departure/accounts display the increased quote.
- Every first-boat purchase, equipment purchase, refuel, repair and licence renewal opens a confirmation with Cancel initially selected. Repayment and arming cover also ask for confirmation. Ordinary boat purchases retain their named confirmation. Insured departure explicitly confirms the premium before sailing. Consumable fuel continues to burn through normal operation; a purchase prompt is not inserted per simulation tick. Choosing owned boats, assigning share-paid crew, and borrowing retain their existing non-purchase behavior.
- New player careers start at day 0 with “A talking to by Frank,” identifying him as the investor and explaining the $20,000 budget, boat options, difficulty, crew, gear and return costs. An isolated, authored 240 m cove has a loan Workhorse, calm water, one marked shelf and one unmarked shelf. Frank is drawn aboard and gives ten short lessons. Actual throttle, turning, zoom, chart opening, location, diver harvesting and recovery advance the relevant lessons. Normal harbour/shopping/season menus stay unavailable during the lesson; orders, cove chart, lesson menu and title remain available.
- Each lesson can be skipped, and the whole tutorial can be skipped from its briefing, aboard panel or controller-accessible lesson menu. Its phase, catch, remaining clumps and diver state survive save/reload. Completing or skipping returns to the funded first-boat choice on day 1, with the full initial budget and fresh crew; day-zero costs/catch/exposure do not enter season accounting. Existing careers and separate Test Mode/prototype sessions do not acquire a tutorial.
