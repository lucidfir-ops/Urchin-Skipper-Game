Prompt: Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production texture atlas for realistic top-down nautical charts in a coastal fishing game. Create one landscape raster atlas with THREE equally wide vertical panels, no gutters: left third softly weathered pale ivory nautical chart paper with fine natural fibre grain; middle third realistic overhead muted turquoise and blue-green open seawater with tiny very subtle wave texture; right third realistic strictly overhead dense Pacific Northwest evergreen forest canopy with fine rocky grey speckles, evenly distributed. Materials are subdued, detailed and naturally varied, designed to be sampled beneath crisp vector chart markings. Flat orthographic, even diffuse lighting, uniform scale, no perspective or cast shadows. Each panel fills its entire third. Absolutely no typography, labels, symbols, frames, coastline, islands, boats, people, depth markings or invented navigation features. This is texture material only; authoritative game geometry will mask the water and forest. Avoid high contrast or large focal objects. Output a clean high-resolution wide atlas.

`src/chart-material.js` blends samples only beneath authoritative terrain masks. `src/chart-art.js` retains existing contours, ground outlines, currents, tracks, selections and legend contrast. The regional schematic retains its original route/coast paths. The artwork never supplies navigation geometry or hidden stock/depth information.
